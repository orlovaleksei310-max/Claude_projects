CLAUDE PROJECTS

A growing collection of games that run right in your browser.

Criminal Runaway

You just robbed the bank. Grab the cash, dodge the cops, and reach the getaway car.

Bank Heist 3D

Third-person 3D. Hold up the teller, set off the alarm, grab the cash and reach the getaway car, then spend the cash at the HQ gun shop and pick your next target: Power Plant (wire puzzle and laser corridor), Factory (guards, stairs, lockpicks) the Casino, where shooting a gambler brings endless police and FBI waves, and finally the Crown Jewel (hack, lasers, vault, helicopter). Desktop only.

Dirt Bike Ride

Third-person 3D. Ride a dirt bike down an endless winding road: lean into the curves, dodge rocks and logs, hit the ramps, pull flips and wheelies, and use the nitro. The biome changes every 1000 m: grassland, desert, snow, city and forest. How far can you get? Desktop only.

Boss Rush

Top-down 2D. You wake up in a cave and fight five bosses in a row: Rock Golem, Bat Queen, Slime King, Spider Matriarch and the Crystal Wyrm. Aim with the mouse, dash through attacks, and pick an upgrade after each kill.
