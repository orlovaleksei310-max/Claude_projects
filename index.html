CLAUDE PROJECTS

A growing collection of games that run right in your browser.

Criminal Runaway

You just robbed the bank. Grab the cash, dodge the cops, and reach the getaway car.

Bank Heist 3D

Third-person 3D. Hold up the teller, set off the alarm, grab the cash and reach the getaway car, then spend the cash at the HQ gun shop and pick your next target: Power Plant (wire puzzle and laser corridor), Factory (guards, stairs, lockpicks) the Casino, where shooting a gambler brings endless police and FBI waves, and finally the Crown Jewel (hack, lasers, vault, helicopter). Desktop only.

Dirt Bike Ride

Third-person 3D. Ride a dirt bike down an endless winding road: lean into the curves, dodge rocks and logs, hit the ramps, pull flips and wheelies, and use the nitro. The biome changes every 1000 m: grassland, desert, snow, city and forest. How far can you get? Desktop only.

Boss Rush

Top-down 2D. You wake up in a cave and fight five bosses in a row, then a lava portal opens to the Underworld and five much tougher bosses, and after the Infernal Dragon an acid portal leads to five more, then a dream portal to the Dreamland then the Shadow Realm, and finally the Land of the Gods (30 bosses). Beat Zeus and a cosmic portal opens Part Two: five planets, 24 more guardians and the World Eater (55 bosses total). Aim with the mouse, dash through attacks, and pick an upgrade after each kill.

NERF: Spin & Splat

Top-down 2D foam-blaster paintball. Spin the wheel for a random blaster (scatter shot, sniper, splat launcher, ricochet, seeker darts and more), then fight room to room across 13 maps (vote on the type before each match: normal, house, village or portals) against AI opponents or real players online (quick match with anyone, or a friends room with a code; you pick a username). Camp a door or rush; the bots do both, and your screen flashes RUSH! when one comes for you. Three hits splat you, first to three round wins takes the match.
